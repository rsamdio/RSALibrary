Rotaract Library does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information beyond what is required for normal web hosting and analytics (such as basic logs and aggregated usage statistics).

Any data you enter into the resource request form is used only to process your request (including spreadsheet storage and confirmation email as configured by the library team). You should avoid entering sensitive personal, financial, or medical information.

If you have questions about how your data may be processed or stored by the hosting provider, please reach out to the contact email listed in the footer of the main site.
