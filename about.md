**Rotaract Library** is a curated library of assets, templates, guides, checklists, and tools for Rotaract leaders. It is published by **[Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO)]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }})** so clubs and district officers can find trusted materials in one place.

### Who it is for

Rotaract club boards, district leaders, and members across South Asia who need ready-to-use brand kits, meeting agendas, role guides, learning materials, and regional identity assets.

### How to use it

1. Browse **Resource Groups** on the homepage or open a group page directly.
2. Preview a file, then use **View** or **Download**.
3. If something is missing, [request a resource]({{ '/request/' | relative_url }}) and the library team will review it.

### How it relates to other [Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO)]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }}) tools

Rotaract Library sits alongside other [Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO)]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }}) initiatives such as Pulse, Rotaract Buddy, NAVIGATE, Publications Hub, and Certify. Those tools support live sessions, questions, pathways, and recognition; this library focuses on downloadable and viewable documents and brand assets.
