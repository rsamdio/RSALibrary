### What is Rotaract Library?

Rotaract Library is a curated library of assets, templates, guides, checklists, and tools for Rotaract leaders, published by [Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO)]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }}).

### Who maintains it?

**[Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO)]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }})** maintains the collection. Contact [{{ site.data.site.publisher_email }}](mailto:{{ site.data.site.publisher_email }}) with questions, or visit [rsamdio.org]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }}).

### Can clubs use official Rotaract brand assets from here?

Yes. See [Rotaract Brand Assets]({{ '/rotaract-brand-assets/' | relative_url }}) for official Rotary family logo files. Use them in line with Rotary International brand guidelines.

### How do I request a missing template?

Open the [resource request form]({{ '/request/' | relative_url }}), describe what you need, and the library team will review it.

### What is the Batch of 26-27 Badge set?

[Batch of 26-27 Badge]({{ '/batch-of-26-27-badge/' | relative_url }}) collects badges and logo assets by [Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO)]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }}) for the 2026–27 Rotary year (PNG, SVG, and master files).

### How is this related to Pulse, Buddy, and other [Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO)]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }}) tools?

The library is the document and asset hub in the [Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO)]({{ site.data.site.publisher_url }}) ecosystem. [Pulse](https://pulse.rsamdio.org/), Buddy, NAVIGATE, Publications Hub, and Certify support other club and district workflows. They work alongside the library.

### Are resources free to use?

Resources are offered for Rotaract use on an as-is, best-effort basis. Treat them as indicative templates and assets, verify before official use, and follow Rotary brand rules when using logos.
